## How I work in a team
### Integration
When joining a team, I'm very carefull about following the rules, policies, design philosophies and tasks which the team has undertaken. I achieve this by reading documentation, asking alot of questions and visualing the patterns of my pairs. My philosophy is that I am a cog in a machine, and I need to adapt to fit inside the machine. 

### Socially
I know the importance of a team's mood and motivation to a product's development. Therefore, I always treat my colleagues with respects and kindness. If I have a complain about a colleague's work, I will go discuss it with them and explain my reasoning in a kind manner.
